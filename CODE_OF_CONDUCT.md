# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Songline to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their
musical experience, background or identity. We pledge to make participation a
harassment-free experience for all.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and beginners
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully
- focusing on what is best for the people using Songline

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private
- sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances
- publishing others' private information without their permission
- other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and
when representing the project elsewhere. To report a problem, contact the
maintainer privately through the contact options on their
[GitHub profile](https://github.com/mbhewitt). Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, or temporarily or permanently ban
contributors, for behaviour that breaks this code.
